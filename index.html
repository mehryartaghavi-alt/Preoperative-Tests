Preoperative Assessment & Investigation

This clinical decision-support tool is designed to assist in the preoperative assessment of patients and to identify appropriate investigations based on the patient's clinical condition, comorbidities, functional capacity, ASA class, BMI, and type and grade of surgery.

A careful clinical assessment should always be performed:

History should focus on:

  • Cardiovascular and respiratory systems
  • Functional capacity / METs
  • Frailty
  • Relevant medications and previous medical conditions

Physical examination should focus on:

  • Cardiovascular system
  • Respiratory system
  • General clinical condition
  • Airway assessment ?
  • OSA assessment (BMI >40) ?
Important Note

The system provides guideline-based recommendations only; final clinical decisions remain the responsibility of the surgical and anesthesia team.
