Preoperative Evaluation & Tests

The preoperative evaluation is the clinical foundation for patient management, reduces perioperative morbidity and enhances patient outcome.

At first, a careful clinical assessment should always be performed:

History should focus on:

  • Vital systems
  • Functional capacity / METs
  • Frailty
  • Medications

🚨 Active Cardiac Condition

Does the patient have an active cardiac condition?

Physical examination should focus on:

  • Cardiovascular & Respiratory system
  • General condition
  • Airway assessment ?
  • OSA assessment (BMI >40) ?
